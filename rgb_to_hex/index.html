Color converter

Convert RGB and hex colors, with optional alpha transparency.

RGB → Hex

Channels: 0–255. Optional alpha: 0–1. Accepts commas, spaces, and rgb() / rgba(). Hex output includes alpha.

Hex → RGB

Accepts 3, 4, 6, or 8 digits, with or without #. Alpha is shown to two decimal places.
